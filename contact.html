Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions before getting started? Here are common answers.

How long does a project take?

Starter single-page sites take about 1 to 2 weeks. Larger multi-page websites and custom applications take 3 to 6 weeks depending on features.

What are your payment terms?

We typically work with a 50% deposit before kickoff and the remaining 50% upon final completion and launch approval.

Will my site be mobile responsive?

Yes, 100%! All our sites are engineered using modern CSS Flexbox and Grid, tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Every package includes post-launch support ranging from 7 days up to 90 days to ensure everything runs smoothly.
